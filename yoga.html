What is Yoga?

Yoga is a holistic science that integrates physical, mental, and spiritual practices. Originating from ancient India, it includes physical postures (Asanas), breath control (Pranayama), meditation (Dhyana), and ethical principles. Yoga aims to unite body and mind, creating balance and harmony in daily life.

Benefits of Yoga

  • Improves flexibility & posture
  • Reduces stress & anxiety
  • Enhances breath awareness
  • Supports mental clarity
  • Boosts immunity & vitality

Yoga Asanas

Tadasana

Mountain Pose: Improves posture, strengthens legs, and promotes grounding.

Balances: Vata

Bhujangasana

Cobra Pose: Strengthens spine, opens chest, improves digestion.

Balances: Pitta

Vrikshasana

Tree Pose: Improves focus and balance, promotes mental grounding.

Balances: Vata

Dhanurasana

Bow Pose: Stimulates abdominal organs and boosts energy levels.

Balances: Pitta

Padmasana

Lotus Pose: The classic meditative pose for calmness and focus.

Balances: All Doshas

Pranayama

Pranayama helps regulate the prana (life force) through breathing techniques. It purifies the mind and body while balancing the three doshas.

Anulom Vilom

Alternate nostril breathing to calm the mind and balance nervous system.

Kapalbhati

Energizing breathing that clears toxins and stimulates digestive fire.

Bhramari

Humming bee breath to instantly reduce stress and improve sleep quality.

Meditation

Meditation is the practice of focused awareness and inner stillness, deepening your connection to the self.

Mindfulness

Observing thoughts and sensations without judgment in the present moment.

Mantra Meditation

Repetition of sacred sounds like "Om" to focus and uplift the mind.

Yoga Nidra

Deep guided relaxation that brings the body into a state of conscious sleep.
