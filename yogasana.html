Overview

In Ayurveda, yoga is recommended not only for physical health but also for balancing the doshas. There are over 84 classical asanas, but Ayurveda emphasizes a set of foundational poses for detox, digestion, and mental clarity.

Common Categories:

Standing Poses
Tadasana, Utkatasana, Trikonasana, Virabhadrasana.
Seated Poses
Padmasana, Siddhasana, Gomukhasana, Paschimottanasana.
Inversions & More
Shirshasana, Sarvangasana, Halasana, Bhujangasana, Savasana.

History & Evolution

The practice of asanas dates back thousands of years to ancient India. Originally, asanas were meditative seated postures used by sages during deep contemplation.

From the Vedic rituals involving chants and fire offerings to the Eight Limbs of Ashtanga Yoga by Patanjali, yoga has evolved into a global science of health.

The Eight Limbs (Ashtanga)
  • Yama: Ethical restraints
  • Niyama: Personal observances
  • Asana: Physical postures
  • Pranayama: Breath control
  • Pratyahara: Withdrawal of senses
  • Dharana: Concentration
  • Dhyana: Meditation
  • Samadhi: Enlightenment

Classical Texts

Ancient scriptures provide the framework of yoga as a spiritual and physical discipline.

  • Patanjali's Yoga Sutras: Defines asana as "steady and comfortable".
  • Hatha Yoga Pradipika: Detailed instructions on 15 core asanas.
  • Gheranda Samhita: Encyclopedic approach describing 32 asanas.
  • Shiva Samhita: Outlines 84 asanas and their spiritual links.

Pranayama & Asana

Asanas prepare the body by removing physical blockages, allowing the practitioner to sit comfortably. Once stable, Pranayama balances energy (prana).

Techniques & Benefits
  • Nadi Shodhana: Balances brain hemispheres.
  • Bhramari: Humming bee breath relieves anxiety.
  • Kapalabhati: Detoxifies the respiratory system.
  • Ujjayi: Soothes the nervous system.
