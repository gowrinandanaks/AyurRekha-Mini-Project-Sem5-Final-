The Foundations of Healing

Ayurveda relies on a deep understanding of nature's countless treasures to heal ailments and keep us physically, mentally, and spiritually at our best. The clinical approach is broadly divided into two main categories:

  • Shodhana Chikitsa (Panchakarma): An all-natural, holistic technique of purifying the body. It takes 21 to 28 days to cleanse the system of toxins deposited in minute body channels (srotas).
  • Shamana Chikitsa: Pacification therapies that use medicated oils, herbs, and lifestyle changes to manage imbalances without deep purification.

Purvakarma: Preparing the Body

Before deep detoxification, the body must be prepared. These techniques loosen toxins and lubricate organs to make the body receptive to further treatment.

  • Snehan (Internal & External Oleation): The digestion of medicated ghee or an external light massage to lubricate internal organs and enhance circulation.
  • Uzhichil / Abhyanga: A highly beneficial daily ritual. It is a deep, full-body massage using large quantities of herb-infused oil to stimulate blood/lymphatic circulation and relieve fatigue.
  • Swedan: Sweating induced through medicated water or milk steam. It activates sweat glands to collect toxins so they can bind with oils for removal.
  • Avagaham: The receiver is submerged in a hot water bathtub infused with powerful herbs. The heat and oils kickstart heavy sweating to expel toxins and relieve severe lower back and hip pain.

Panchakarma: The Core Detox

These five major therapies target specific organs to profoundly cleanse the body's systems.

  • Vamanan (Emesis): Medically induced vomiting using special herbal concoctions. Focuses on the respiratory system and upper GI tract to cure Kapha diseases like asthma and skin issues.
  • Virechanam (Purgation): Medically induced purgation to purify the digestive tract, liver, and pancreas. It resets the digestive system and eliminates Pitta-related skin and eye diseases.
  • Snehavasthy (Oil Enema): Conducted after a meal, this medicated oil enema deeply cleanses and lubricates the large and small intestines, curing Vata and spinal disorders.
  • Kashayavasthy (Herbal Enema): Conducted before a meal, this herbal decoction enema nourishes the lower digestive tract and cures chronic rheumatisms and neurological disorders.
  • Nasyam: Medicated oil droplets are introduced into the nose to clear out blockages above the neck. Highly effective for sinuses, scalp diseases, and brain/spinal cord disorders.

Targeted Healing Therapies

These highly effective treatments target specific areas of the body suffering from inflammation, pain, or functional loss.

  • Tharpanam: Specially medicated ghee is poured and pooled over the eyes. It improves eyesight, relieves stress from screen time, and repairs ocular damage.
  • Kateevasthy: A boundary of black gram dough is created on the lower back and filled with warm medicated oil. It stimulates circulation to treat stiffness, osteoporosis, and chronic back pain.
  • Dhanyamladhara: A continuous stream of acidic, herb-infused liquid (made from cereals and vinegar) is poured over the body. It works wonders for arthritis, gout, and inflammatory pain.
  • Upanaham: A thick herbal paste is applied to an affected joint or localized area and left overnight (or up to 12 hours) to slowly absorb and cure severe swelling and ulcers.
  • Rakthamoksham (Bloodletting): A powerful technique to remove small quantities of impure blood (often using leeches) to resolve severe toxic buildup and vital organ disorders.

Rejuvenation & Metabolic Boost

These therapies are famous for toning the body, rebuilding muscle tissue, and profoundly calming the mind.

  • Udwarthanam (Powder Massage): A vigorous dry massage using coarse herbal powders (sesame, horse gram). It awakens the lymphatic system, burns subcutaneous fat, reduces cellulite, and promotes weight loss.
  • Ksheeradhara: Pure cow's milk infused with sandalwood and vetiver is poured in a continuous stream over the forehead. It deeply pacifies Pitta dosha, curing insomnia, anxiety, and memory issues.
  • Navarakizhi: A massage using warm cotton bundles filled with medicinal Navara rice cooked in milk. It activates blood circulation and heals nerve degeneration, strokes, and muscle wasting.
  • Narangakizhi: A specialized, powerful bundle massage utilizing the medicinal properties of lemons to relieve severe joint pain and lower back issues.
