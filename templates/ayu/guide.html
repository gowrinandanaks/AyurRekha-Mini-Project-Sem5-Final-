Introduction to Ayurveda

Ayurveda, often called the "Mother of All Healing," is a 5,000-year-old system of natural healing that has its origins in the Vedic culture of India. More than just a system of treating illness, Ayurveda is a science of life (Ayur = life, Veda = science or knowledge).

It teaches us that health is a state of equilibrium between the body, mind, and spirit. By understanding your unique constitution and aligning your lifestyle with the rhythms of nature, you can achieve profound and lasting wellness.

A Brief History

The wisdom of Ayurveda was passed down orally for generations before being recorded in the Vedas. The three main classical texts—the Charaka Samhita, Sushruta Samhita, and Astanga Hridaya—remain the foundations of Ayurvedic practice today, covering everything from internal medicine to surgery and rejuvenation.

Mind, Body & Spiritual Constitution

Prakriti vs. Vikriti

Your Prakriti is your original genetic constitution at birth. Vikriti is your current state of imbalance. Ayurveda works to return you from Vikriti to your natural Prakriti.
