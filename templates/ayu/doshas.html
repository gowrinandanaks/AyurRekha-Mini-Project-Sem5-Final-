What is a Dosha?

According to Ayurveda, every human being is born with a unique proportion of five elements: Earth, Water, Fire, Air, and Space. These elements combine to form the three primary life forces, or Doshas. Together, they govern all our physical and mental processes.

VATA

Air + Space

Vata: The Force of Movement

Vata is the energy of movement. It governs all biological activity, from the blinking of eyes to the beating of the heart and the flow of thoughts in the mind.

Physical

Light, thin build, dry skin, sensitive to cold. Rapid speech and movement.

Mental/Emotional

Creative, imaginative, quick to learn but quick to forget. Propensity for anxiety or fear when imbalanced.

PITTA

Fire + Water

Pitta: The Force of Transformation

Pitta governs digestion, absorption, and metabolism. It is responsible for all biochemical transformations in the body and produces heat.

Physical

Medium build, warm skin, strong digestion. Likely to have red or blonde hair and freckled skin.

Mental/Emotional

Intellectual, focused, and ambitious. Tendency toward anger, irritability, or perfectionism when imbalanced.

KAPHA

Earth + Water

Kapha: The Force of Stability

Kapha provides the structure, lubrication, and stamina for the body. It forms the skeletal system, fluids, and joints, and provides psychological stability.

Physical

Solid, strong build. Soft, oily skin and thick, lustrous hair. Generally enjoys excellent stamina.

Mental/Emotional

Calm, patient, and loving. Loyal and steady. When imbalanced, can become possessive, lazy, or depressed.
