Frequently Asked Questions

What is AyurRekha?

AyurRekha is a wellness platform that offers tools and knowledge based on Ayurveda to help you understand your Dosha, follow a balanced lifestyle, and discover healing practices.

How do I take the Dosha Quiz?

Visit the Dosha Quiz page, answer all 10 questions, and click Submit. You'll be shown your Dosha and get suggestions based on your profile.

How do I download the Dosha Report?

After completing the quiz, you’ll be redirected to the Dosha Report page. Click the "Generate PDF" button to download your personalized report.

Do I need to log in?

Yes, login ensures your answers and reports are securely stored. AyurRekha uses cookie-based login sessions for smooth access.

What kind of questions can I ask the chatbot?

You can ask questions about Ayurveda, yoga, health tips, dosha information, and guidance related to the website.

Is my conversation with the chatbot private and secure?

Yes, your chat is kept private and is only used to help answer your questions and improve your experience.

The site is not loading correctly. What should I do?

Try refreshing the page or clearing your browser cache. You can also contact us using the details below for help.
