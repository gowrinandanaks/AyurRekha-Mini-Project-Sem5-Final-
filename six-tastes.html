Balance in Every Bite

In Ayurveda, taste is not just for pleasure; it is a clinical indicator of a food's effect on our physiology. Each taste (Rasa) is made of two primary elements and has a direct impact—either pacifying or aggravating—on the three Doshas.

🍯

Madhura

Earth + Water

Nourishing, grounding, and building. Found in grains, dairy, and sweet fruits.

Decreases: Vata & Pitta

Increases: Kapha

🍋

Amla

Earth + Fire

Stimulates digestion and circulation. Found in citrus, vinegar, and yogurt.

Decreases: Vata

Increases: Pitta & Kapha

🧂

Lavana

Water + Fire

Enhances flavor and softens tissues. Found in sea salt and kelp.

Decreases: Vata

Increases: Pitta & Kapha

🌶️

Katu

Fire + Air

Stimulates metabolism and clears sinuses. Found in chilies, ginger, and garlic.

Decreases: Kapha

Increases: Vata & Pitta

🥬

Tikta

Air + Space

Detoxifying, anti-inflammatory, and cooling. Found in leafy greens and turmeric.

Decreases: Pitta & Kapha

Increases: Vata

🍏

Kashaya

Earth + Air

Drying and firming. Found in beans, green tea, and pomegranate.

Decreases: Pitta & Kapha

Increases: Vata
